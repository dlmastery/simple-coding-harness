## What a classroom result means

- A **mechanism exercise** runs a small idea on a teaching task.
- A **replay** uses recorded outcomes and cannot supply missing environment evidence.
- A **numerical illustration** exposes a calculation without reproducing a trained system.
- A **simulation** uses explicitly constructed rules or values.
- A **result audit** checks a published claim against its methods and reported evidence.
- A **reproduction** requires an executed, sufficiently faithful protocol and a record of differences. No frontier paper has been reproduced in the current authoring evidence.

The studio identifies its mode before the action. Large-model training is optional and requires a source-aligned implementation, appropriate hardware, data access, and an explicit resource plan.
