Choose one runnable, licensed task family and record the input assets, task versions, reward criteria, judges, and data permissions. ## Checkpoints and pairing

Every run identifies model, harness, task set, reward/evaluator, source, dependencies, seed schedule, and budget. A resumable checkpoint includes optimizer and scheduler state, random states, progress, data order, and the versions required by the chosen framework. Weights alone may be insufficient. Keep training attempts distinct from evaluation attempts.

Evaluate old/new models with old/new harnesses under matched tasks and attempts. Preserve all four pairs, including regressions. Choose harnesses using development results, then freeze the pair before final evaluation. Do not combine local wine measurements, invented pair-table values, and real training results into one score.
