# Dream-RSI classroom boundary

Inspected 21 September 2026: [Dream-RSI v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14858v1), dated 14 September, sections 2 and 3 through policy selection. This is selected-method reading, not a full-paper reproduction audit.

The paper's policy selects batches of eligible root or leaf nodes. Online attempts extend saved workspaces. Replay reveals recorded continuations: the next root child or a non-root node's recorded child. Its objective combines quality, represented attempts, and parallelism. Policy revisions are compared on fixed history, then the selected code returns online. The underlying discovery model and evaluator remain fixed.

Our labs use a simpler recipe-ancestry tree, fixed node orders, a two-attempt allowance, and lower MAE. They omit saved-workspace inheritance, source eligibility transitions, parallel batches, its objective, and iterative policy-code development. B and C share baseline A in the teaching record; that shape is not an implementation of the paper's non-root continuation rule. The separate online task tests these classroom policies only.

The transferable distinction is recorded evidence versus new execution. The exercise cannot establish the paper's reported performance, cost savings, or full recursive improvement mechanism.
