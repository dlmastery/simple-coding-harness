# Scaffolding source audit

Read [Environments as Scaffold, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08404v1), published 8 September 2026, on 21 September. Inspected the preliminary definitions and sections 3.1–3.3; the experiment overview supplies the training context.

The paper distinguishes advice about the next action from added information about the current state. It also varies when feedback arrives within an episode and across training. Its reported learning involves reinforcement learning and evaluation without the training scaffold.

Lab 10.33 preserves the distinction between the two kinds of help. Four constructed form tasks expose a stale-hint detour. They omit model training, long-horizon environments, independent contexts, and the paper's evaluation. A correct unassisted form is not evidence that guidance entered model weights.
