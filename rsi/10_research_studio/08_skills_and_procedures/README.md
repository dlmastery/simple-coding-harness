- [10.28 · Refine a procedure graph](step_28_procedural_graph/README.md): A small procedure graph with one tested transition edit and retained rejected proposals.
- [10.29 · Repair a skill for an experiment-results page](step_29_gui/README.md): A local results page, one interaction trace, and a revised inspection skill.
