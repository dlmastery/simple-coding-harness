# Two different uses of retained information

Selected primary reading on 21 September 2026. These sources are within the preceding month; their methods are not interchangeable.

## WikiSkill

[WikiSkill v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27454v1), 27 August 2026, sections 3.1–3.2.4. The method separates raw traces, persistent wiki knowledge, and active skills. Training rollouts use active skills with wiki access restricted, while maintenance and proposal roles use the accumulated knowledge. A failed skill update can be rolled back without rolling back the wiki. Our two-check exercise preserves that retention distinction and logs scripted reads. It does not reproduce multi-agent evolution, held-out validation, or an isolated coding-agent information boundary.

## S3Gym

[S3Gym v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.31100v1), 31 August 2026, method overview and the History ICL versus Summary Memory analysis. It studies how agents test, judge, and incorporate experience, comparing raw-history use, summarized memory, and parameter-training pathways under an evaluation protocol. Our arithmetic packets address only the information preserved by two external representations. Five shared-context author answers on one constructed sequence do not reproduce its exploration, self-judging, held-out tasks, or weight training. Packet length alone is not a measure of learned ability or provider cost.
