## Metadata-only selection rule

Download suite membership, task definitions, dataset descriptions and quality
metadata. Preserve the original responses with retrieval URLs and SHA256 hashes.
Do not fetch published model scores or run models during this inventory.

Mark a task as a laptop candidate when metadata reports 750–30,000 rows,
5–65 columns including the target, and an explicit CC0, CC-BY or public-domain
license. Missing metadata or an ambiguous license stays unresolved. These
conditions bound preparation cost; they do not predict learnability or gain.

Sort eligible tasks by numeric task ID within each suite. Nominate the first
six of each as a twelve-task candidate panel, subject to raw schema, provenance,
duplicate/group structure and licensing checks. Do not replace a task because
a model score is low, high or inconvenient. Document a technical exclusion and
the next eligible replacement before fitting. Retain the full inventory,
including ineligible tasks and the reason.

Before any training, declare development and task-transfer roles, split rules,
all procedure comparisons, budgets, metrics, uncertainty and failure policy.
The candidate panel is not yet that final experiment protocol. Preserve
classification/regression and preprocessing needs. Published cross-validation
tasks must not be described as fully reproduced if a laptop adaptation uses
fewer folds or capped data.

This inventory does not finish the open Recuris, RSIAgent or MetaSkill repairs.
Their planned experiments still need actual memory/update ownership, retained
skills, later execution and fixed external comparisons. Students will invoke
