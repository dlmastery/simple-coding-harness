Teaching point: A better inner researcher may still fail in the outer improvement role.Moving a discovered researcher into the role that improvesresearchers changes the task. Success at model search does not guaranteesuccess at harness design. The source audit records that the reported ignitionefficiency difference was not statistically significant.
