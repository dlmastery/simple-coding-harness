Teaching point: The solver does the task. The improver changes its procedure. The evaluator judges the resulting work.Use one concrete ML search as the example. A model parameterbelongs to the solution. An experiment-allocation policy belongs to theresearch procedure. A rule that chooses among revisions to that policy belongsto the improver. The external comparison stays fixed while internal procedureschange.
