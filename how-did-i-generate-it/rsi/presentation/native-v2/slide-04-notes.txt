Teaching point: An input must exist when the prediction is made.The bike component counts sum to total demand. Giving bothcomponents to the predictor reveals the answer. Ask students to identify theshortcut before explaining it. Observed weather also changes the question:retrospective demand estimation is different from forecasting tomorrow withunknown weather. State the intended use before interpreting accuracy.Source: lab 00.01 and the bike data card.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/00_start_here/step_01_meet_the_task/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/target-leakage-v2.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
