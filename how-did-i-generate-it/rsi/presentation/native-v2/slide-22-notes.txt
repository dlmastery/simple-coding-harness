Teaching point: Each method changes a specific part of scientific work.Use the map to locate the methods rather than asking studentsto memorize all names. The important questions are what changes, what feedbackdrives the change, and what later evidence supports it. The studio includessource reading as well as experiments. A paper's reported result, a classroomadaptation and an independent reproduction must remain distinct.Source: theme 10 overview and research sweep.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/10_research_studio/README.mdhttps://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/research/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/research-studio-map-v3.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
