Teaching point: Reliable behavior depends on how the components work together.Improvements can come from coordination even when componentmodels stay fixed. A planner may request a candidate that a tool cannot build.A checker may validate the wrong output. Trace one request across the systemand show the joins that keep its identity intact. Later, when componentschange, we must retest their combined behavior.Source: theme 05.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/05_system_intelligence/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/system-coordination-v3.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
