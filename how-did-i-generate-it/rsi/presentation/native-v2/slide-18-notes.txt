Teaching point: A later improvement round must use the revised improver.Saving a revised file establishes that a proposal exists.Loading it later establishes source use. A trace showing its rule governinga different decision adds behavioral evidence. A fair comparison then askswhether the resulting process improves new work. Keep these observationsseparate. A short lineage can demonstrate the mechanism without establishingsustained acceleration or broad autonomy.Source: labs 09.03–09.06 and MetaSkill method audit.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/09_recursive_self_improvement/step_04_inherit/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/inherited-improver-v1.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
