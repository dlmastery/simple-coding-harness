Teaching point: A recorded tree supports policy experiments without repeating every fit.A policy can ask to start again from the root or continue anobserved leaf. Replay exposes the recorded continuation. It cannot supply ascore for an untried branch. In the laptop adaptation, the inner proposer isa fixed program and the coding agent revises the exploration policy.
