Teaching point: A training-median predictor gives a simple reference for regression.Work through the small illustrated example before discussinglarger models. The predictor learns its constant from training rows. We thenmeasure error on different rows. A weak baseline helps teach the metric, butan effectiveness study also needs strong baseline procedures. Beating thetraining median does not by itself show that a research agent improved.Source: lab 00.03 and the figure's illustrative-number caption.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/00_start_here/step_03_one_attempt/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/training-median-baseline-v1.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
