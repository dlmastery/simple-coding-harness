Teaching point: A hypothesis connects an observed error to a proposed experiment.Ask students to name one change and what observation wouldcount against it. A record that says only “try a better model” gives littleinsight into the decision. Keep the old prediction file. A failed candidateis useful evidence when its assumptions and cost remain visible. At thisstage, the research procedure stays fixed.Source: early experiment labs and run-ml-experiment skill.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/02_loop_engineering/step_01_why_repeat/README.mdhttps://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/skills/run-ml-experiment/SKILL.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/one-factor-model-change-v1.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
