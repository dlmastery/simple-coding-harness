Teaching point: Explain each changed object. Run a bounded experiment. Verify later use. Judge quality and cost on new work.The practical goal is to inspect an improvement claim withoutrelying on its label. By the end, students should identify the object thatchanged, the evidence that motivated the change, and the later comparison thattests it.
