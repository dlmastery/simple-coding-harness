Teaching point: A scientific claim needs experiments, ablations and a response to criticism.A plausible hypothesis is the start of a scientific process.Ask which observation would distinguish its mechanism from a simplerexplanation. A review score supplies feedback, but its meaning depends on whoor what produced it. Distinguish automated review from publication acceptanceand independent replication. Connect each revised claim to the experimentthat changed the evidence.Source: ScientistTwo, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19644, and scientist-method audit.Course evidence and reading:https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/10_research_studio/06_scientist_two/README.mdhttps://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/evidence/2026-09-21/scientist-labs/README.mdIllustration: https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/7db77bd8d38aa5f23dc4180e341de415d6b99296/rsi/assets/illustrations/scientific-claim-v2.pngThe figure explains a mechanism.
