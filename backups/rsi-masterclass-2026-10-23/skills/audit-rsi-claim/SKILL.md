# Audit an RSI claim

Read the claim and its primary evidence. List what changes, what stays fixed, who supplies feedback, what persists, who approves promotion, and what the next run actually uses.

Classify the mechanism before its effectiveness: temporary correction; retained memory or learning; solver improvement under a fixed improver; organizational change; self-modification; inherited revision to an improvement procedure. These are overlapping dimensions, not a single ranked ladder.

For structural recursion, trace an improver revision into a later improvement round. For effective improvement, compare the resulting improvement process with its predecessor under matched conditions. For acceleration, demand evidence across generations and total resources. State which claim the evidence reaches.

Inspect leakage, evaluator access, selection versus final cases, rejected edits, transfer, uncertainty, and missing costs. A protected-looking filename is not access control. An automated reviewer is not a real conference decision. Separate reported paper results from locally executed measurements.

For a paper, record title, version, date, authors, affiliations, source, evidence type, method, data, metric, resources, ablations, and limitations. Read the relevant methods before teaching a causal explanation. Label abstract-only leads accordingly. Discovery queries must target the preceding month; older requested foundations stay clearly dated.

Return a readable `CLAIM-AUDIT.md` with supported claim, missing evidence, one counterexample, and the smallest useful next experiment. Do not invent an author statement, result, or negative finding from inaccessible material.
