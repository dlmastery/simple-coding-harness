# Dated announcement search

Executed 21 September 2026 for lab 10.02. Window: 22 August–21 September inclusive. All queries used explicit dates and the tool's 31-day recency filter. Results outside the window were rejected as recent releases. This was a bounded refresh of an existing corpus, not an exhaustive survey.

Exact submitted queries:

```text
site:research.google/blog ScientistTwo September 2026 after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
site:x.com ("RSI" OR "self-improvement") ("Meta" OR "FAIR") after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
site:deepmind.google ("recursive self-improvement" OR "Dream-RSI") after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
"ScientistTwo" "Nam" after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
"Dream-RSI" site:x.com after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
"ScienceBuddy" announcement after:2026-08-21 before:2026-09-22
```

The first group surfaced the Google Research index and older official material, including June's From AGI to ASI and older ERA/SIMA work. Their original dates exclude them from new releases here. The second group found [ScientistTwo's project](https://scientist-two.github.io/), [its arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19644v1), [the author's homepage](https://jaehyun513.github.io/), and [PhAI's ScienceBuddy announcement index](https://phai-labs.com/en/news/). ScientistTwo was selected for the single complete trail. ScienceBuddy remains an existing corpus entry, not a new paper count.

No canonical original X thread was returned for the two social searches. A third-party profile mirror was not used to attribute an original post. This establishes a search gap, not absence of social announcements. No guessed post URL was invented and no inaccessible thread was claimed as read.

Primary reading followed project → arXiv → methods/results, with identity checked through the author's homepage. Project gallery access did not yield a verified downloadable example through the text reader. This limitation is retained in the claim card.

A first tool click on Weco's companion post failed to resolve its arguments; retrying with the returned page reference opened the official page. This was a tool-resolution failure, not evidence that the website was inaccessible. Framework reading used the requested historical Weco July material separately from current-month discovery.
