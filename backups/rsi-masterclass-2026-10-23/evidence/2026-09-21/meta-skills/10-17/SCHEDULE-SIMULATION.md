# Hypothetical schedule accounting

Eight task rounds. Task round cost: one invented unit. Meta update cost: two invented units.
Horizon 1: eight updates; total 24 units.
Horizon 2: four updates; total 16 units.
Horizon 4: two updates; total 12 units.

A longer horizon delays the next opportunity to revise the updater while spending fewer assumed update units. Responsiveness, result quality, and noise are not measured. A shorter schedule also changes the updater more often, making attribution harder unless snapshots and evaluation intervals are explicit. Zero fits or task decisions were added by this arithmetic simulation.
