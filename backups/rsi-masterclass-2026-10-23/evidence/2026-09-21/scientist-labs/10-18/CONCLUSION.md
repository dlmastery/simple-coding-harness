# A conditional predictive result

Both fits and separate prediction checks completed. Calendar-only selection MAE is 109.807667751. Adding observed weather gives 99.175923787. The decrease is about 9.68%, which exceeds the predeclared 5% threshold in this exposed-data repeat.

The supported statement is: for the supplied Ridge recipe trained on 2011 bike records and scored on January–June 2012 with MAE, the permitted observed-weather feature group improved prediction relative to calendar-only inputs. This does not establish causation, advance forecasting accuracy, or transfer to another place or period.

The added fields do not remove the large commuting-hour errors. That limitation motivates the next study's estimator changes. The retained artifact is the linear/all recipe. The researcher instructions remain unchanged; its context now includes these results.
