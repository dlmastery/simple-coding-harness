# Fixed coordinator

Ready requires a recognized task and unambiguous target. Reserve one fit before entering running. A successful fit enters awaiting-check, not complete. Complete requires a successful checker packet for this exact task, candidate, and prediction digest. Missing or mismatched evidence stays awaiting-check. Ambiguous target enters needs-clarification; only a real clarification can resolve it. No human approval is fabricated.

One baseline fit; two main failure fixtures. The additional wrong-candidate fixture is separately declared and uses no fit.
