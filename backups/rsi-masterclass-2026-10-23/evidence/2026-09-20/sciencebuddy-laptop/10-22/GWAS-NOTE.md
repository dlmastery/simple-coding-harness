# From association to a scientific question

A genome-wide association study compares variation across genomes with a trait. The [NHGRI glossary](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Genome-Wide-Association-Studies-GWAS) provides the term's basic context. This is a background definition, not a recent research claim.

Invented example: a dataset shows that carriers of a variant are, on average, one centimetre taller. That observation alone does not show that changing the variant changes height. Population structure, correlated variants, sampling, and other factors could explain part of the relationship. The observed association suggests questions for additional analysis; it does not prescribe an intervention.

A domain reviewer must assess the design, multiple testing, confounding, replication, and relevance of a proposed follow-up. This exercise has no human genomic data, association calculation, biological finding, or clinical recommendation. Its purpose is to distinguish a measurable association from a stronger causal claim.
