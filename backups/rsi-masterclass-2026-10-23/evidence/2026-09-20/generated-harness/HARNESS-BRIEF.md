# Brief for the generated bike harness

Build a small CPU research harness for retrospective hourly bike-rental estimation. Use the pinned course data and the established chronological partitions. Predict `cnt`; exclude `casual` and `registered`. Calendar and observed weather are available under this teaching setting. This is not a day-ahead forecasting claim.

Use training-only preprocessing and MAE. Start with the training-median baseline. Permit a later explicit model hypothesis, but admit at most two attempts in a workspace, including failed attempts. Keep predictions, proposal, score, timing, versions, and failures. Use the supplied course tools where they meet this brief.

Generate runnable implementation, readable workflow and recovery notes, dependency information, and a separate result check. The student gives natural-language instructions. Prove one baseline, one leaked-input refusal, and refusal of a third request. Do not run an autonomous search or change the evaluator to accept a candidate.
