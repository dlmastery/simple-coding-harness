# Read a Frontier Claim

**Group 00 · October 2026 Research Focus**  
**Your place:** Which claim does the available evidence support? 2 labs; 0.5–1.0 hours.

## Purpose

Before implementing October 2026 RSI mechanisms, learn to distinguish what a paper actually demonstrates from what it might suggest. This skill protects you from misinterpreting headlines as proven results.

- State what remains untested in a paper
- Explain why a laptop adaptation preserves mechanism but not scale
