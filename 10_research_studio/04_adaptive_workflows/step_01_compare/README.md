# 10.12 · Compare Workflow Adaptation

**Purpose:** Distinguish RSIFlow (meta-agent orchestration) from Self-HPO (harness-policy alternation).

## Comparison

| Aspect | RSIFlow | Self-HPO |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Focus | Workflow orchestration | Harness + policy |
| Adaptation | Meta-agent evolves bottlenecks | Policy proposes harnesses |
| Iteration | Continuous workflow adjustment | Alternating H-step/P-step |
