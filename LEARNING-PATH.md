# Learning Path

Recommended routes through the RSI masterclass based on your goals and available time.

---

## Quick Reference

- **Quick Start**: 5 hours, themes 00-02 + Budget RSI demo + one capstone
- **Standard Course**: 30 hours, themes 00-09 + selected October 2026 groups + capstones
- **Full Masterclass**: 60 hours, complete course + all October 2026 + all capstones

---

## Path 1: Quick Start (5 Hours)

**For**: Beginners who want a fast introduction  
**Outcome**: Understand dependable ML loops and see one RSI mechanism

### Session 1 (2 hours): One Prediction
- Theme 00: Start with a prediction
  - Lab 00.01: Meet the prediction task
  - Lab 00.02: Prepare workspace
  - Lab 00.03: Run one baseline
  - Lab 00.04: Check evidence

### Session 2 (1.5 hours): Dependable Loops
- Theme 01: Make one process dependable
  - Lab 01.01: Write the process
  - Lab 01.02: Run the process
- Theme 02: Repeat for a reason
  - Lab 02.01: Why repeat
  - Lab 02.02: Bounded state

### Session 3 (1.5 hours): October 2026 + Capstone
- October 2026 demo
  - Run Budget RSI demo (30 min)
- Capstone
  - Capstone 11.01: Build a harness from brief (1 hour)

**Checkpoint**: You can explain one prediction, its evidence, and a bounded loop.

---

## Path 2: Standard Course (30 Hours)

**For**: Students who want full foundations  
**Outcome**: Complete RSI understanding with October 2026 research

### Week 1 (8 hours): Foundations
- Theme 00: Start with a prediction (2 hours)
- Theme 01: Make one process dependable (2 hours)
- Theme 02: Repeat for a reason (2 hours)
- Theme 03: Give different cases different routes (2 hours)

### Week 2 (8 hours): Advanced Foundations
- Theme 04: Agree on what the experiment means (2 hours)
- Theme 05: Build capability around the model (2 hours)
- Theme 06: Generate a harness from a brief (2 hours)
- Theme 07: Separate the self-* ideas (2 hours)

### Week 3 (8 hours): Measurement and RSI
- Theme 08: Measure what improved (2 hours)
- Theme 09: Improve the improvement procedure (2 hours)
- October 2026: Reading frontier research (1 hour)
- October 2026: Budget RSI (1 hour)

### Week 4 (6 hours): Research Studio and Capstones
- October 2026: Second-order diagnostics (1 hour)
- October 2026: Scaling coordination (1 hour)
- Capstone 11.01: Build a harness (2 hours)
- Capstone 11.02: Recursive comparison (2 hours)

**Checkpoint**: Complete Themes 00-09, October 2026 groups 00-02, two capstones.

---

## Path 3: Full Masterclass (60 Hours)

**For**: Researchers who want deep RSI understanding  
**Outcome**: Complete mastery with all October 2026 papers and capstones

### Weeks 1-4: Same as Standard Course (30 hours)

### Week 5 (8 hours): Complete October 2026
- October 2026: Reading frontier research (1 hour)
- October 2026: Budget RSI (1.5 hours)
- October 2026: Second-order diagnostics (1.5 hours)
- October 2026: Scaling coordination (1.5 hours)
- October 2026: Adaptive workflows (1.5 hours)
- October 2026: Environments and metrics (1 hour)

### Week 6 (8 hours): Capstones
- Capstone 11.01: Build a harness (2 hours)
- Capstone 11.02: Recursive comparison (2 hours)
- Capstone 11.03: Portability (2 hours)
- Capstone 11.04: External audit (2 hours)

### Week 7 (8 hours): Advanced Topics
- Paper deep-dives (2 hours)
- Mechanism analysis (2 hours)
- Peer review (2 hours)
- Teaching back (2 hours)

### Week 8 (6 hours): Final Projects
- Project work (4 hours)
- Presentations (2 hours)

**Checkpoint**: Complete all themes, all October 2026 groups, all capstones.

## Choosing Your Path

**Choose Quick Start if:**
- You have limited time
- You want a quick overview
- You're not sure if RSI is for you

**Choose Standard Course if:**
- You want solid foundations
- You plan to use RSI in practice
- You have 1-2 months available

**Choose Full Masterclass if:**
- You want deep understanding
- You plan to do RSI research
- You have 2-3 months available
